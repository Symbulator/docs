Question.

Let's say you have three resistors. R2 and R3 are in paralell and R1 is in series w/ the paralell branch (R1 is on the top of the page and the paralell branch is under R1). The voltage level on top of R1 is 12 V. you want .25mA to go throuh R3. R1 is 30 K and R3 is 5 K. What is R2?

Answer.

Clean r2.

DelVar r2

Using a one-line command in Symbulator:

sq\dc("e1,1,0,12; r1,1,2,3E4; r2,2,0,r2; r3,2,0,5E3") :solve(ir3=.25E-3,r2)
          r2=11520.7
