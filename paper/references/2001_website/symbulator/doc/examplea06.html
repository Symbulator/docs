Solution:

"e1,1,0,100*d(t),0; l5,1,2,5,0; l6,2,3,6,0; r6,2,0,6,0; r5,3,0,5,0; r4,1,4,4,0; l4,4,3,4,0; m3,l5,l4,3,0; m2,l4,l6,2,0"à cir
