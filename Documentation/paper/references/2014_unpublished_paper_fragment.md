Symbolic simulation of linear circuits with Symbulator

Engineering students around the world have to learn the basics of
linear circuit analysis as part of their formation. Circuit theory
requires students to learn a set of mathematical techniques to find
the solution they seek. However, circuit analysis is not - and should
not be - about the mathematical apparatus it requires: the focus
should be on understanding the circuits that are being analyzed. To
paraphrase RW Hamming, "The purpose of circuit analysis is insight,
not mathematics."

Back in 1999, as a senior engineering student, I developed a symbolic
circuit simulator whose purpose was to take care of the mathematics of
solving a circuit, so that students like me could focus instead on
understanding the circuit as a whole. This program, called Symbulator,
has been for the last 15 years the premier symbolic simulator of
linear circuits, used and appreciated by hundreds, perhaps thousands
of engineering students. A new version has been released.
